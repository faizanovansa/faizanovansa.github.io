About Me

I'm Faiza, a Visual Communication Design student at a vocational high school in Indonesia. What started as curiosity about how websites work evolved into a passion for building beautiful digital experiences.

My approach combines design thinking with development skills. I believe that great websites aren't just functional—they should feel thoughtful and intentional. Every pixel, every animation, every interaction matters.

Currently, I'm diving into web development, exploring AI possibilities, and learning cybersecurity fundamentals. I'm inspired by anime worldbuilding and storytelling—not the characters, but the way those worlds feel alive through atmosphere and detail. I try to bring that same sense of wonder to the things I create online.

I value honesty and authenticity. I'll never claim experience I don't have. Instead, I'm always learning, always building, always curious.
