About Me

I'm Faiza, a Visual Communication Design student with an obsessive appreciation for beautiful interfaces. I started exploring web development because I wanted to understand how great design actually works when you make it interactive.

My journey spans visual design (DKV), frontend development, and recently diving into AI and cybersecurity. I believe the best digital experiences come from understanding both the art and the engineering—knowing how to balance aesthetics with functionality.

I'm drawn to clean, minimal interfaces, beautiful color theory, and the kind of atmospheric worldbuilding you find in great anime stories. That inspires how I approach design: thoughtful, intentional, never flashy.

Currently learning, always building, eternally curious about what's possible when you combine code and creativity.
