About

Still learning, on purpose.

I'm currently a Vocational High School student majoring in DKV (Visual Communication Design), which means I think about screens from two directions at once — how they look, and how they work.

My path into code started with curiosity, not ambition. I build things to understand them: a small AI tool, a messy script, a layout that finally clicks. Cybersecurity entered the picture the same way — not as a career plan yet, just a question I keep pulling on.

I'd rather show honest progress than a polished résumé. Nothing here is inflated. If a project says "currently building," it means exactly that.

Design Philosophy

Calm is a feature.

"Clarity before cleverness."

If a design needs an explanation to be understood, it isn't finished. I try to remove before I add.

"Motion should earn its place."

Every animation on this site responds to something — a hover, a scroll, an intent. Nothing moves just to look busy.

"Honesty is a design constraint."

No fabricated projects, no borrowed experience. What you see is where I actually am right now.
